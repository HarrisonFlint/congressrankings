Methodology

Normal-person version

Each competitor has a rating estimate and an uncertainty estimate. The estimate, μ, represents the model’s current best guess of competitive strength. The uncertainty, σ, represents how unsure the model still is. A competitor with strong results and many rounds will have a higher μ and lower σ. The public ranking is sorted by exposure instead of raw μ, as exposure penalizes uncertainty. This controls entries who have few, but successful, rounds from ranking extremely high.

On a similar note, competitors with less than 4 rounds are filtered out in order to prevent overreaction from tiny sample sizes (and to control for chamber noise due to parent judges if ykwim!!)

Math-person version

$$ R_i = \mathcal{N}(\mu_i, \sigma_i^2) $$ $$ \text{Eligible}_i \iff \text{rounds}_i \ge 4 $$ $$ r_{i,t} < r_{j,t} \Rightarrow i \succ_t j $$ $$ \mu_i', \sigma_i' = \operatorname{TrueSkillUpdate} \left( R_i, \succ_t, \beta, \tau \right) $$ $$ \text{Exposure}_i = \mu_i - k\sigma_i $$ $$ L = \{i : \text{rounds}_i \ge 4\} $$ $$ i \prec j \iff \text{Exposure}_i > \text{Exposure}_j, \quad i,j \in L $$ $$ \text{Rank}_i = 1 + \left| \{j \in L : \text{Exposure}_j > \text{Exposure}_i\} \right| $$

Variable definitions

\(i, j\): competitors. \(t\): chamber being processed. \(R_i\): competitor \(i\)'s rating distribution. \(\mu_i\): estimated skill. \(\sigma_i\): rating uncertainty. \(r_{i,t}\): placement of competitor \(i\) in chamber \(t\). \(\succ_t\): ordered result list for chamber \(t\). \(\beta\): performance-variance parameter. \(\tau\): rating-drift parameter. \(k\): uncertainty penalty used for exposure. \(L\): pool after eligibility filter.
